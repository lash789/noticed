About Mila

Mila was always hurrying.

Not because she had to…
but because her mind was always somewhere else.

Thinking about what just happened.
Or what might happen next.

Until one day, she learned something simple…
and everything changed.

Mila and the Moment Catcher isn't just a children's book. It's a gentle reminder, for kids and adults, that the moments we're searching for aren't somewhere else. They're already here.

In the sound of water running.
In the warmth of the sun.
In the quiet feeling in your chest when you finally slow down.

Mila's story is the beginning of something bigger — a small, growing movement rooted in noticing.

Noticing the ordinary.
Noticing the beautiful.
Noticing yourself.

Because the truth is…
there's nothing you need to chase.

The magic was never the net.

The magic is that you can pause, look around, and say —
"I see you."

The Heart Behind It

This space was created as a place to practice that.

To slow down.
To catch moments.
To share them with someone who understands.

Whether you're here as a parent, a reader, or just someone who needed a breath today — you're part of it.

So… have you caught a moment today?
